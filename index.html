Vacation Scheduler

Android Vacation Management Application

Vacation Scheduler helps users organize vacations, manage excursions, schedule reminders, and generate vacation summary reports.

Application Features

  • Create, edit, and delete vacations
  • Manage excursions
  • Search saved vacations
  • Generate vacation summary reports
  • Schedule notifications
  • Share vacation information
  • Store data locally on the device
